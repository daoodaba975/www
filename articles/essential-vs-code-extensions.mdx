# My Essential VS Code Extensions

<MdxImage
  src="/blog/03.essential_vs_code_extensions/cover.webp"
  alt="Cover"
  width={960}
  height={540}
/>

<MdxLink href="https://code.visualstudio.com" name="Visual Studio Code" /> is one
of the most popular editors among developers. It’s lightweight, fast, and above all,
highly customizable thanks to its vast <MdxLink
  href="https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/vscode"
  name="extension"
/> ecosystem.

<br />
Over time, I’ve tested countless extensions, some framework-specific, others more
general. Today, I’m sharing the ones I consider my essentials, the ones I always
install first on every setup.

## Dependi – Smart dependency manager

Keeping dependencies up to date can quickly become a hassle, especially in large projects.

<MdxLink
  href="https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=fill-labs.dependi"
  name="Dependi"
/> makes it simple: you can see outdated packages, check for vulnerabilities, and
update them in no time.

<br />
💡 It saves me time and ensures my projects stay secure and up to date.

## Error Lens – Errors at a glance

<MdxLink
  href="https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=usernamehw.errorlens"
  name="Error Lens"
/> : no longer need to dig through the console to find errors. They appear directly
in the editor, right next to your code, with clear visual highlights.

<br />
💡 Debugging becomes faster and more visual, making it impossible to miss an error.

## Git Graph – Visualize your commits

Navigating Git history can be confusing, especially with multiple branches.

<MdxLink
  href="https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=mhutchie.git-graph"
  name="Git Graph"
/> provides a clean, visual representation of your commits, branches, and merges.

<br />
💡 It helps me quickly understand a project’s history and easily compare changes
between commits.

## Prettier – Format your code

A true classic 🔥

<MdxLink
  href="https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=esbenp.prettier-vscode"
  name="Prettier"
/> automatically formats your code, keeping it consistent: indentation, quotes, semicolons,
and more.

<br />
💡 The result, I can focus on what really matters : coding.

## Todo Tree – Organize your TODOs

We’ve all left "// TODO" comments in our code.

With <MdxLink
  href="https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=Gruntfuggly.todo-tree"
  name="Todo Tree"
/> : they’re no longer forgotten: the extension gathers them all in a dedicated sidebar
view.

<br />
💡 It helps me stay organized and ensures I don’t miss important improvements or
tasks.

## Supermaven – My favorite AI copilot

I’ve tested several AI copilots, but <MdxLink
  href="https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=supermaven.supermaven"
  name="Supermaven"
/> is the one that convinced me the most.
Its code suggestions are smarter, faster, and more relevant than most alternatives.  
The <MdxLink
href="https://supermaven.com/pricing"
name="Pro"
/> version at $10/month is in my opinion a better investment compared to others.

<br />
💡 It boosts my productivity with high-quality suggestions while staying lightweight.

## In conclusion ✅

These are my essential VS Code extensions, tools I use every day no matter the project.  
They help me write cleaner code, stay productive, and simplify my workflows without depending on any specific technology.
